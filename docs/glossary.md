# Glossary

- **Available at:** earliest timestamp a value could actually have been used by the system.
- **Candidate lineage:** exact combination of dataset, configuration, strategy, and model hashes.
- **Correlation/causation ID:** identifiers connecting a decision to its workflow and immediate cause.
- **Drawdown:** decline from a prior equity peak.
- **Embargo:** unused interval after a split boundary to prevent label overlap leakage.
- **Eligibility:** evidence gate result permitting operator review, not trading authority.
- **Meta-labeler:** model that scores a signal already generated by a deterministic rule.
- **Paper venue:** simulator producing fills and positions without contacting a broker.
- **Profit factor:** gross profit divided by absolute gross loss.
- **Purge:** removal of training examples whose label horizon overlaps validation/test.
- **Risk at stop:** expected loss from entry to stop, including size and contract specification.
- **Stressed costs:** replay with spread, slippage, and commission doubled.
- **Untouched test:** chronological holdout opened once for a candidate lineage.
